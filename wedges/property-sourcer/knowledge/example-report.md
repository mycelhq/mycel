**Verdict: pursue** — 3-bed terrace, Bolton BL3, £150,000

- Achievable rent: £995/mo (£11,940/yr). Comparable: 2× let within 0.3mi at £975–£1,025 (source:
  local listings, achieved).
- Gross yield: **7.96%**.
- Running costs: mgmt £1,194 + maint £1,194 + insurance £300 = £2,688/yr.
- Purchase costs: SDLT £5,000 + legal £1,500 + survey £600 = £7,100.
- **Net yield: (11,940 − 2,688) ÷ (150,000 + 7,100) = 5.89%.**

**Risks**
- Net yield 5.89% is just under the 6.0% box — marginal. A £5k price reduction clears it.
- EPC is D; a future C requirement could mean ~£3–5k spend.
- Kitchen dated; budget £2k or reflect in offer.

**Next steps**
