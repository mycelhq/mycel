# Sourcing criteria (the buyer's box)

Seed values — the founder edits these live via the knowledge API as the mandate changes.

## The box (default buyer)
- Region: North West England (Manchester, Salford, Bolton, Rochdale).
- Price: £90k–£220k.
- Minimum **net yield: 6.0%**. Gross is a screen; net is the decision.
- Prefer 2–3 bed terraced houses. Flats only if lease > 100 years and no cladding issues.

## How we calculate (be consistent)
- **Annual rent** = monthly achievable rent × 12. Use comparable *achieved* rents, not asking.
- **Gross yield** = annual rent ÷ purchase price.
- **Running costs** (annual): letting/management 10% of rent, maintenance 10% of rent, insurance
  £300, plus service charge + ground rent for flats. Assume no void in year 1 unless flagged.
- **Purchase costs**: SDLT (BTL surcharge), legal £1,500, survey £600, sourcing fee if any.
- **Net yield** = (annual rent − running costs) ÷ (purchase price + purchase costs).

## Red-line risks (auto-`pass` or flag hard)
- Lease under 85 years, or any cladding / EWS1 uncertainty.
- Flood zone 3, subsidence history, non-standard construction.
- Sitting tenant (unless the brief wants tenanted stock).
- Area with structurally falling rents or oversupply.
