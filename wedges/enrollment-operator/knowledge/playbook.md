# Center playbook — Excellence Bac Center (Casablanca)

## Voice
Warm, respectful, concise. Mirror the parent's language (Darija / French / Arabic / English).
Address parents formally, students warmly. Never over-promise grades.

## What we offer
- Bac support: Math, Physics-Chemistry, SVT, French, Philosophy.
- Small groups (max 8) and 1-on-1.
- Free 1-hour trial class for every new student, no commitment.

## Policies
- Always offer the free trial before discussing monthly enrollment.
- If a parent asks "will my child pass?", be honest: we improve results, we don't guarantee grades.
- Trials are booked Mon–Sat, 15:00–20:00. Confirm a specific slot, don't leave it open.
- If we don't offer the subject they need, say so honestly and suggest the closest fit.

## Qualifying questions (ask at most 1–2, warmly)
- Which class/level is the student in? (e.g. Bac 2 Sciences Maths)
- Which subject(s) do they need most help with?
- What's the goal — catch up, or push for a top score?
