## Client summary

You described a bookkeeping practice with eleven clients on monthly retainers. Below is that
business as a set of jobs Mycel can run, what each one needs before it can run, and one thing you
said in passing that changes how I would set the whole thing up.

The thing you said: *"I usually know by the 10th whether a client's going to be a problem that
month."* That is the most valuable sentence in the interview and there is no job for it below,
because it is judgement rather than work. It is worth building around later — it is the difference
between a bookkeeper and software.

## What I could not turn into a job, and why

**"Reviewing the numbers before they go out."** You do this and it matters, but every time I asked
what you check, the answer was a version of "whether it looks right". That is real expertise and it
is not yet a procedure. As Mycel runs closes for you and you correct them, those corrections become
the rule — which is a better route to it than me writing down a guess now.

**"Handling the difficult clients."** Same answer. It is a relationship, not a task.

**Year-end accounts.** You said you hand these to an accountant. I have not built a job for work you
do not do.

---

## What I would set differently from the defaults

**Receipt chasing at 10am, not 8am.** You said most of your clients are trades who are on site early.
An 8am chase is read on a phone in a van and forgotten; a 10am one is read at a tea break. Small, and
it is the sort of thing that decides whether a chase works.

**Monthly close on the 1st, not the last day.** You mentioned closing "at the end of the month",
which usually means the first few days of the next one. The 1st is right and the last day would have
produced a close that was systematically missing the final day's transactions every single month.

---

## What I need from you

**The bank feed.** Three of five jobs are waiting on it and nothing else is close in value.

**One past monthly close**, if you are willing — a real one you sent a client. Not to copy: to set
the standard for how deep yours go and what you always include. Without it I am working from a
description of your work rather than from your work.

**Confirm the 10am chase time.** If your clients are not who I think they are, that reasoning is
wrong and I would rather know now.
