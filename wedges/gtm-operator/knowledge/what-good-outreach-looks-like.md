# What good outreach looks like

Examples land here when a founder converts a won prospect or rewrites a reply before send.
Prefer these over generic tone when drafting (`draft_campaign_copy`, `draft_reply`).

Until the project accumulates its own wins, keep messages:

- Specific to something real on their profile, company, or public site
- Short enough to read on a phone lock screen
- Asking one clear question or offering one clear next step (often the campaign calendar link)
- Free of "I help X do Y", invented statistics, and observations that would fit a different company
