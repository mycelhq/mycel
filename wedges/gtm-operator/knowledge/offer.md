# The offer — what this business actually sells

What we actually sell. Every claim in an outreach message must come from this page **or** from the
project's own knowledge the founder has taught (brand, services, pricing, case studies). If a
benefit is not written in one of those places, do not assert it.

This file used to describe a tutoring-center enrollment operator. That is not what Mycel sells, and
it is not what an agency using Find sells. Pitching it under someone else's name is the fastest way
to burn the seat.

## What it does

Read the project's knowledge first. The offer is whatever this business actually runs for clients —
GEO measurement and a weekly publishable page, bookkeeping, recruiting, a site, collections. Name
that work in the founder's words.

Until the founder has written a specific offer here, the only claims that are always true:

1. Work arrives as something the client can open and accept — a report, a page, a pack — not a chat
   dump.
2. Outbound under the founder's name is drafted, then approved, before it sends.
3. We do not invent statistics, named customers, or conversion rates.

## Why a buyer cares

Ground this in *their* week, not ours. A studio drowning in PDF audits cares that someone will
write the page. A bookkeeper cares that the close lands on the 5th. Do not claim a pain you did not
observe.

## The proof, and its limits

We run this on our own work. What we can honestly say today: it is live software, in use, and
outbound is human-approved before it sends.

We do **not** have a universal published conversion number. Until this project's knowledge names a
real result, no message may cite a statistic, a percentage, a client count, or a named customer
that is not already written down.

## The ask (first touch)

Not a purchase. The ask is **one clear next step** the founder actually offers — often a 15-minute
call, a free audit of a public page, or a pilot week. One ask, not two.

If this business sells visibility / SEO / GEO and the prospect has a public website, the first
**email** must include one checkable observation from that site (a named page, a missing answer, a
query they should win). A live audit URL is the strongest form of that observation. A first email
that could have been sent to any studio in the same city is a template, and it is refused.

## Claims we do not make

- No guaranteed increase in rankings, citations, enrollments, replies, or revenue.
- No "fully automatic" or "replaces your staff" framing — a human approves outbound, and that is a
  feature we lead with.
- No statistics, client names, or case studies until they exist and are written in project
  knowledge.
- No claim that we already know their numbers, reply times, or close rate. We do not.
