# ICP — who this business sells to

Find runs under the **founder's** name, for **their** buyers. The wedge we are currently selling is
whatever this project actually installed — not a leftover tutoring-center operator.

Do not pitch a business that fails the hard filters below, or that fails the filters in this
project's own knowledge (`icp`, brand, services). When those disagree, the project's knowledge wins.

## The buyer

Whoever this business's shape names: the owner or the person who can say yes in one conversation.
There is usually no procurement process and no CRM. The decision takes one conversation.

Typical Mycel buyers (when this *is* Mycel's own seat): an owner-operated service business — SEO /
GEO studio, bookkeeping practice, recruiting desk — that already does the work by hand and is
drowning in the same week every week.

Typical agency buyers (when this is an agency's seat): whoever *they* sell to. Read their knowledge.
Do not substitute ours.

## Hard filters (fail any → `disqualified`)

- Not a buyer of what this business actually sells. A tutoring center is out unless this business
  sells to tutoring centers.
- No public contact point that the business itself published. If we would have to guess or scrape a
  personal number, it is out. See `outreach-playbook.md`.
- Obviously inactive: no post, review, or update in ~12 months.
- Already running a competing operator they are happy with, when that is visible.

## Tiering

| Tier | Looks like | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Fits the offer, owner-operated, recent public activity, a pain we can point at on their site or profile | Feels the pain and can decide alone |
| **B** | Real fit, slower (larger team, or the pain is inferred not visible) | Worth a touch after the A list |
| **C** | Minimal presence, or unclear whether they still operate | Only when A/B is exhausted |

## Buying signals (raise the score)

- A public page that should answer a question and buries it.
- Reviews or posts mentioning slowness, missed reports, or work that never ships.
- Ads running (they are paying for leads, so wasting them hurts more).
- Unanswered inbound sitting on a channel they published.

## Anti-signals (lower the score)

- Already advertising an automated assistant they are proud of.
- Very large multi-city chains when this business sells to owner-operators — wrong buyer, wrong cycle.

## What we do not know from a list

A sourced record almost never tells us: volume, who answers, close rate, or whether they have tried
this. These belong in `missing_info`, never in an assumption. It is correct to say "we do not know
their volume" and pick an angle that does not depend on it.
