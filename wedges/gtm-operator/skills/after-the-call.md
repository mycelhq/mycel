# After the call

Mycel notes can sit in the room and file what was said. That is not a client, and it is not an
engagement.

## Decide

- **Go** — they are buying. Put them in the book, open delivery, ask for a brief if the call was
  thin. Do not invent a deposit on an empty intake.
- **Hold** — they are a client, waiting on a brief or the first job they name. No delivery case.
- **Pass** — not this time. They stay a prospect. No client, no case.

A booking or a reply without a meeting can still put them in the book. Do not open delivery on a
stranger you just emailed.

Do not start work because a meeting was marked booked.
