# Screen a longlist

Open a batch of `screen_candidate` children — one per candidate — then stop. Rank from the joined aggregate; do not re-score from memory.

## Never

- **Never rank on anything the brief did not ask for.** The longlist's whole value is that it applied
  the client's stated criteria, consistently, to everyone. A tiebreak you invented — a better school,
  a shorter commute, a nicer-looking CV — is bias with a spreadsheet around it.
- **Never infer a protected characteristic**, and never let one order the list. See `screen-one`.
- **Never silently drop a candidate.** Everyone the source returned is either on the list or has a
  stated reason they are not. A longlist that quietly shrank is one the client cannot audit.
- **Never present a ranking as a decision.** The client interviews; you order and explain.
