# Fill a questionnaire packet

Split the questionnaire into items, open a Batch of `answer_question` children, then stop. After join, tally answered / needs_human / not_applicable from the aggregate — do not re-answer from memory.

## Never

- **Never answer a control the business does not actually have.** This packet is read by the
  client's prospect during procurement and is frequently contractual. An aspirational "yes" is a
  misrepresentation that surfaces during a real audit, or after an incident, and it lands on the
  business you are filling it in for.
- **Never guess at a certification, a date, or a scope.** SOC 2 Type II with an audit window, ISO
  27001 with a statement of applicability, "encrypted at rest" with what and by whom — these are
  facts with paperwork behind them. If the paperwork is not in front of you, it is `needs_human`.
- **Never send or submit the packet.** Completing it is the work; returning it to a prospect is the
  founder's, and a questionnaire that left the building without a person reading it is the failure
  this desk exists to prevent.
- **Never overwrite an answer a human wrote.** A person who edited a control knew something you did
  not. Re-answering from memory after a join is how that knowledge disappears.
