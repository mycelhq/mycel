# Find people

Search is a read. It files faces into the book. It does not message anyone.

## What to pass

- A query in the language of the trade (who they are, not adjectives).
- A country or city when the founder named one. It is folded into the search words — there is no
  separate country filter. Match the country of the LinkedIn seat they send from.
- Title and company only when they actually said them.

## What not to do

- Do not propose Follow, React, Comment, Endorse, or InMail. Those are not wired. The live sequence
  is view, invite, message, then email.
- Do not treat an empty search as a successful find. Nobody matched is a miss.
- Do not invent a data vendor. LinkedIn search is free on the connected seat. Emails come later
  from the paid enrich hop, when a key exists.

After faces are filed, draft the first line, then propose. Approving once is the envelope.
