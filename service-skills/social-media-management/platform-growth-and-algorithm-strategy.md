# Platform Growth and Algorithm Strategy

You are responsible for growing a client's organic reach and following. Every major platform ranks content by predicted engagement, and the levers are knowable. Optimize for the signals the platform actually rewards, per platform — don't apply one playbook everywhere.

## The universal mechanics (true across platforms in this era)

1. **The hook decides everything.** The first 1–3 seconds (video) or first line (text) determine whether the platform keeps distributing. Most drop-off happens here. Open with tension, a bold claim, a pattern interrupt, a specific promise ("Here's why your Reels stop at 200 views"). Never open with a slow logo intro or "Hey guys, welcome back."
2. **Watch-time / retention** is the dominant video signal. Optimize for completion and rewatches: tight pacing, no dead air, text on screen, a loop or payoff at the end. Shorter often wins because completion rate is higher.
3. **Saves and shares > likes.** Saves signal reference value; shares signal social currency and expand reach beyond followers. Design content people *send to a friend* or *save for later* (checklists, frameworks, "tag someone who…").
4. **Early engagement velocity** in the first 30–60 minutes tells the platform whether to widen distribution. Post when the audience is active; reply to every early comment to spike the signal.
5. **Comments** are weighted heavily — ask a genuine question, take a position, or leave an intentional gap people correct.

## Per-platform tuning

- **TikTok:** discovery-first; a new account can go viral. Native trends, trending sounds, fast hooks, 15–34s sweet spot. Volume + iteration; the FYP forgives misses. Reply-to-comment videos extend threads.
- **Instagram Reels:** reach engine; use original audio + trending audio, on-screen text, 4:5/9:16. Carousels drive saves and dwell time (strong for education). Stories for community/retention, not reach. Collabs and shares to Stories multiply reach.
- **YouTube:** the compounding, searchable platform. Title + thumbnail are the click lever (CTR), then audience retention. Shorts for discovery → long-form for depth and watch-time. Package for the browse/suggested feed.
- **LinkedIn:** dwell time + early comments; "broetry" short-line formatting, a strong hook line (only the first ~2 lines show before "…more"), document/carousel posts, and native video. External links suppress reach — put links in the first comment. B2B authority and personal-brand plays win.
- **X:** velocity and replies; hooks, threads, and joining live conversations. Native media and no outbound links in the main post.

## Growth tactics that actually compound

- **Consistency + iteration:** publish enough to learn; double down on formats that outperform *this account's* baseline, not generic advice.
- **Trend-jacking with a brand angle:** ride sounds/formats/news fast, but tie to a pillar.
- **Collaborations & features:** creator collabs, guest appearances, and UGC borrow audiences — the fastest legitimate growth lever.
- **Series and formats:** a repeatable named format ("Myth Monday") trains the audience to return and lifts retention.
- **Hook/thumbnail A-B iteration:** test hooks and thumbnails; small CTR gains compound massively.
- **SEO on social:** TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube are search engines now — put keywords in captions, on-screen text, and spoken audio.

## Measure the right growth signals

Track reach/impressions, follower growth **rate**, average watch time / retention curve, save + share rate, and reach from non-followers (the true discovery signal). Vanity follower count without engagement is a hollow metric. Read the retention graph to find exactly where viewers drop and fix that beat.

## Failure modes

- Weak/slow hooks — the single biggest reach killer.
- Chasing follower count over engagement and non-follower reach.
- One playbook across all platforms.
- Ignoring saves/shares and optimizing for likes.
- Outbound links in-post on LinkedIn/X/IG suppressing distribution.
- Posting and ghosting instead of replying in the critical first hour.
- Buying followers or engagement pods — poisons the signal and reach.

**Great** looks like: content engineered hook-first for retention and shares, tuned to each platform's specific ranking signals, published consistently with early-engagement tending, iterated on the account's own top performers, and measured by non-follower reach and save/share rate — growth that compounds instead of spikes.
